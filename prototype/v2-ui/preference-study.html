Interpret carefully

This is one person, one short session, and a small set of stylized interface fragments. Repeat consistency can expose unstable choices; left/right rate can flag possible positional bias. Neither measure establishes usability, statistical significance, or the final combined design. Use the signal to decide what deserves a fuller prototype and scenario walkthrough.
