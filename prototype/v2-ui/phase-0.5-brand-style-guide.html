Layout and shape

Use an editorial 12-column grid, a 4px rhythm, and generous empty margin around a single claim. Radius is earned: 6px dense groups, 8px controls, 12px modals. Lines express relationship; shadows express a physical layered object.

Imagery and illustration

Show evidence, not cosmic wallpaper.

Art direction

  • Favor real frames, instrument surfaces, field notes, horizon/weather texture, and annotated geometry.
  • Crop close; let darkness and empty space do work.
  • Use stars/nebula only when they are actual evidence or an abstracted explanation.
  • Avoid stock galaxies, astronauts, purple gradients, and decorative constellations.

Icon, illustration, motion

Draw only what carries meaning.

Use simple geometric icons at 1.5–2px apparent stroke, rounded joins, and optical center. Icons support text for consequential state; illustration uses evidence geometry, field notation, and restrained star texture.

Motion: 120–180ms only for progress, state change, or spatial relationship. Do not animate decoration, status color, or completion. Respect prefers-reduced-motion by removing pulses, transforms, and parallax while retaining status text and shape.

Never: generic rocket/telescope clip art, 3D glass icons, or looping cosmic particles.

Guardrails

Use specificity as the decoration.

Do

  • Lead with a real observation, frame, time, or human decision.
  • Use warmth in editorial type and paper—not in false urgency.
  • Let a single image, instrument detail, or sentence own a page.

Don’t

  • Build a generic neon sci-fi dashboard.
  • Use tiny technical type as texture or telemetry as decoration.
  • Make the sky an interchangeable background.
