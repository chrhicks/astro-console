Brand thesis

A quiet instrument for people who care enough to look closely.

Practical, intimate, and evidence-led. Avoid command-center theatre, stock-nebula spectacle, and startup optimism. The personality is a trusted field notebook with a precise optical instrument inside it.

Selected V1 mark · Alignment Aperture

A field of view, not an ornament.

Desired meets solved.

The V1 symbol links center to observation. A primary body is intentionally cropped by the reticle’s inner field while its companion remains separate.

Minimum: 16px symbol. Clear space: one symbol radius. Use ink on paper, light on night, and mono only for one-color reproduction.

Keep the field-of-view crop and companion separation intact.Do not redraw, animate, or turn the trajectory into a generic orbit ring.Use the versioned package; future mark changes require brand governance.
