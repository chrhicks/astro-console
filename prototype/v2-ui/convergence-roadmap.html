Stop criteria

Prototype discovery is complete when…

  • One walkthrough covers plan, acquire, capture, warning, recovery, switching, review, process, reconnect, and control transfer.
  • Every consequential UI action has a command, eligibility and approval rule, result, and typed failure.
  • Healthy, delayed, warning, failure, recovery, reconnect, stale-client, and ownership traces execute deterministically.
  • The three technical spikes end with recorded measurements and decisions.
  • No unresolved choice can materially change the first local-web foundation slice.

Promote

Interaction specification, domain/contract schemas, scenario traces, state ownership, and reusable deterministic fixtures.

Retain as evidence

Selected reference prototypes and the decision log.

Discard or archive

Alternative layouts, prototype-only interaction code, duplicated static data, and visual polish that has no production owner.

The next phase begins from a frozen reference—not from every prototype at once.
