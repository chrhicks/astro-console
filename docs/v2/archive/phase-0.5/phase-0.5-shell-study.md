# Phase 0.5 Outer-Shell Study

Status: **current comparison instrument — not design authority**

Updated: July 23, 2026

This is the next inquiry from the
[reference audit](phase-0.5-reference-audit.md). It tests the shared frame
around V2's accepted native working objects. It does not alter the Plan,
Acquire, Run Authority, or Process interaction models.

## Question

What persistent shell makes a dense personal observatory workspace feel
specific and calm before the operator reaches its timeline, evidence frame, or
image canvas?

The comparison keeps the same representative run, warning, controller, and
working-object content. A direction succeeds only if its framing improves the
first scan without making the shell itself the product.

## Fixed Evidence

Every direction carries the same minimum projection:

1. **Current run:** target, phase, progress or bounded activity, and health.
2. **Attention:** one meaningful consequence and its owning workspace.
3. **Authority:** current controller or explicit read-only/current capability.
4. **Native work:** an observing-window timeline, solve evidence, or Process
   canvas/history—not a substitute dashboard.

The study omits prototype apparatus: gate badges, scenario selectors, research
questions, and non-operational banners are not candidate production shell
patterns.

## Directions

### A. Instrument Panel

The frame reads as a calibrated observatory instrument: a thin indexed edge,
a stable run register, and quiet sections separated by measurement-like rules.
It earns specificity through orientation, current run identity, and a visible
relationship between attention and the source workspace—not through faux
hardware decoration.

**Risk:** becoming a decorative sci-fi console or making every datum look
equally critical.

### B. Observing Log

The frame reads as an active observing record: time, target, activity, and
evidence form a composed operational log, with navigation secondary to the
current night's trace. The shell establishes continuity across Plan, Observe,
Library, and Process without pretending they are one homogeneous dashboard.

**Risk:** over-literal chronology can compete with workspace-local timelines
and image history.

### C. Field Desk

The frame reads as a restrained field workstation: a compact utility spine,
an anchored activity strip, and intentionally varied material for the current
working surface, supporting evidence, and optional inspection. It is the least
literal direction and must prove specificity through hierarchy and operational
language.

**Risk:** it may remain noun-swappable if its run/attention projection is not
structurally distinctive.

## Review Walkthrough

For each direction, ask before discussing visual preference:

1. In one scan, can an operator identify what the observatory is doing and the
   evidence-bearing object worth looking at?
2. Is an interruption bounded—does it say what is protected, what continues,
   and where recovery lives?
3. Does controller/capability remain legible without turning presence into
   authority or duplicating controls?
4. Would removing V2's run and evidence nouns leave a generic product shell?
5. Does the frame recede once the native object needs judgment?

Record comprehension, then preference. Select one direction or a named,
evidence-based combination; do not average the three into generic polish.

## Decision Boundary

- **Fixed:** accepted inner working objects, ownership, freshness, authority,
  consequential-state behavior, and read-only phone model.
- **Under study:** shell geometry, navigation treatment, active-run/attention
  projection, materials, density, typography rhythm, and priority contrast.
- **Next after selection:** document the chosen rules and validate them against
  the shared awkward-state walkthrough before updating the Phase 0.5 guide.
